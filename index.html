How a new pool goes in

The model follows the five stages of one of our builds as you scroll. The photos beside each stage are from real Graphite Pools jobs.

  1. 1

    Dig and set out

    We mark the pool's shape on site and excavate to the planned depth, keeping the levels true so the finished pool sits flush with your paving.

  2. 2

    Build the shell

    Walls and floor are built and plastered into a solid shell, with steps or a bench where you want them. Pipes for the pump, skimmer and returns go in before anything is closed up.

  3. 3

    Coping, tile band and finish

    Coping goes on around the edge, a band of tiles runs along the waterline where scum lines would stain, and the interior gets its smooth coloured finish.

  4. 4

    Paving and surrounds

    Pavers are laid out from the pool edge on a level, compacted bed, along with any feature wall, planter or path that finishes the space.

  5. 5

    Fill, balance and hand over

    We fill the pool, start the pump and filter, balance the water and show you how everything works. Then it's yours.
